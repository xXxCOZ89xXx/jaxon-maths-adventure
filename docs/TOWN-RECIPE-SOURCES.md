## Tomato pasta

Serves 2. Preparation 10 minutes; cooking 12 minutes; total about 22 minutes. A steady boil for the pasta.

| Ingredient | Required for 2 | Usable contents per pack | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pasta | 200 g | 500 g usable per pack | required |
| Ripe tomatoes | 300 g | 300 g usable per pack | required |
| Basil | 5 g | 15 g usable per pack | required |
| Olive oil | 10 ml | 250 ml usable per pack | pantry |

Tap water: 1000 ml for this batch.

Optional: 20 g grated cheese per 2 servings (milk); 20 g pitted olives per 2 servings.

1. Wash the tomatoes and basil; ask an adult to cut the tomatoes.
2. Mix the prepared tomatoes, torn basil and measured oil into a fresh sauce.
3. An adult cooks the measured pasta in boiling water, following its packet, then drains it.
4. Combine the warm pasta and tomato sauce; add an optional topping if suitable.

Allergen awareness: Wheat/gluten in pasta; milk if cheese is added.

## Mild chickpea coconut curry

Serves 2. Preparation 10 minutes; cooking 20 minutes; total about 30 minutes. A gentle simmer for the sauce.

| Ingredient | Required for 2 | Usable contents per pack | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chickpeas, drained | 240 g | 400 g tin / 240 g drained usable | required |
| Chopped tomatoes | 200 g | 400 g usable per pack | required |
| Coconut milk | 200 ml | 400 ml usable per pack | required |
| Dry basmati rice | 125 g | 500 g usable per pack | required |
| Ground cumin | 1 teaspoons | 20 teaspoons usable per pack | pantry |
| Turmeric | 0.5 teaspoons | 20 teaspoons usable per pack | pantry |

Tap water: 250 ml for this batch.

Optional: Chilli or hotter curry paste: optional adult addition only; not included in this mild recipe or shopping list..

1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas; measure the tomatoes, coconut milk and mild spices.
2. An adult warms these ingredients together and lets the sauce simmer gently.
3. Cook the measured rice with the measured water, following its packet; start it while the sauce simmers.
4. Check the rice and sauce are ready together, then serve. No chilli is needed.

Allergen awareness: Check spice and optional paste labels for mustard, milk or gluten. Coconut is an ingredient too.

## Vegetable flatbread pizza

Serves 2. Preparation 15 minutes; cooking 12 minutes; total about 27 minutes. 200 °C conventional / 180 °C fan.

| Ingredient | Required for 2 | Usable contents per pack | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flatbreads | 2 items | 4 items usable per pack | required |
| Passata | 100 g | 500 g usable per pack | required |
| Spinach, thawed and drained | 100 g | 250 g usable per pack | required |
| Pepper | 80 g | 160 g usable per pack | required |
| Mozzarella | 100 g | 125 g usable per pack | required |

Optional: Basil leaves to finish; A little grated hard cheese (milk).

1. Ask an adult to preheat the oven and prepare the pepper; squeeze excess water from the thawed spinach.
2. Cover the flatbreads with the measured passata, vegetables and torn mozzarella.
3. An adult bakes two flatbreads per batch until hot throughout and the cheese has melted.
4. Let the pizzas cool slightly before an adult cuts them for serving.

Allergen awareness: Wheat/gluten in flatbreads and milk in mozzarella; check flatbread labels for other ingredients.

## Carrot and red lentil soup

Serves 2. Preparation 10 minutes; cooking 25 minutes; total about 35 minutes. A gentle simmer.

| Ingredient | Required for 2 | Usable contents per pack | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carrots | 250 g | 500 g usable per pack | required |
| Red lentils | 100 g | 500 g usable per pack | required |
| Onion | 100 g | 200 g usable per pack | required |
| Garlic | 1 cloves | 6 cloves usable per pack | required |
| Vegetable stock cubes | 1 items | 6 items usable per pack | pantry |
| Olive oil | 10 ml | 250 ml usable per pack | pantry |

Tap water: 750 ml for this batch.

Optional: Chopped parsley to finish.

1. Wash the carrots; an adult chops the carrots, onion and garlic. Rinse the lentils.
2. An adult softens the onion and garlic in the measured oil, then adds the carrots.
3. Add the lentils, stock cube and measured water; simmer until the vegetables and lentils are tender.
4. Serve as a chunky soup, or let an adult blend it safely away from splashes.

Allergen awareness: Stock cubes may contain celery, gluten or milk: check the label.

## Banana oat pancakes

Serves 2. Preparation 10 minutes; cooking 15 minutes; total about 25 minutes. Medium heat.

| Ingredient | Required for 2 | Usable contents per pack | Group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rolled oats | 100 g | 500 g usable per pack | required |
| Oat milk | 125 ml | 1000 ml usable per pack | required |
| Eggs | 2 items | 6 items usable per pack | required |
| Bananas | 1 items | 3 items usable per pack | required |
| Baking powder | 2 teaspoons | 20 teaspoons usable per pack | pantry |
| Cooking oil | 5 ml | 250 ml usable per pack | pantry |

Optional: 0.5 teaspoon vanilla extract per 2 servings; Fresh fruit or yoghurt to serve (yoghurt contains milk).

1. Measure the oats and oat milk; peel the banana and crack the eggs into a separate bowl.
2. An adult helps blend the oats, milk, banana, eggs and baking powder into a smooth batter.
3. An adult lightly oils a pan and cooks two small pancakes at a time, turning them when set.
4. Cook both sides until the centre is set; serve with a suitable optional topping.

Allergen awareness: Eggs; oats and oat milk may contain gluten. Optional yoghurt contains milk.

All recipes: Wash hands and produce. An adult must handle sharp knives, hot pans, boiling water, the oven and any blender. Timings are estimates: an adult checks the food is properly cooked. Check every ingredient and topping label with an adult, including possible cross-contact. These notes do not guarantee allergy safety.
