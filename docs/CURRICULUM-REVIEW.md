# Major upgrade: educational review and implemented foundations

Development checkpoint, 22 October 2026. The live game remains on `release/ipad` at `2e84e74`; this is not a full curriculum/release acceptance claim.

The source is the [DfE mathematics programme of study for England](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study), including the Years 3–6 and KS3 sections. KS3 specifies a key-stage programme, not separate national Year 7/8/9 lists. [CURRICULUM-MAP.json](CURRICULUM-MAP.json) maps all original 55 skills and eight new foundations to relevant curriculum strands, prerequisites and outstanding gaps. Each record deliberately says **partial**. Town financial/workplace topics are practical applications/extensions, not separate statutory coverage claims.

There are **63 skills**, not 63 fully covered curriculum objectives. Thirteen have expanded generators; the other fifty retain their existing banks pending the full upgrade. Their existing mastery policy remains identifiable rather than being silently relabelled as the newer standard. New snapshots/evidence fields are additive. Existing independent/practice evidence is retained when appending sessions; no eight-record truncation is applied by the updated engine.

Examples of conceptual variation: grouping versus sharing versus a remainder that needs another vehicle; an incorrect claimed sum versus finding a missing addend; reconstructing a whole from a unit fraction; distinguishing decimal length from magnitude; reading a chart key rather than counting symbols. A countable crate-array model supports equal-group practice. Numerical entry/choice remains appropriate when calculating an exact result is the task.

Before full release, expand and review the remaining banks, strengthen topic-specific teaching beyond the current foundations, and implement the still-missing curriculum areas. Notably incomplete are formal written-method depth, inequality/function/graph work, geometrical constructions/nets/similarity/Pythagoras/trigonometry, grouped and bivariate statistics, and wider probability models. The current generators are not a certified months-long complete Years 3–9 course. Foundation retrieval has finite mathematical fact sets; long-term scheduling/coverage needs acceptance across realistic usage, not artificial question-ID suffixes.

Compatibility checks use synthetic records, including a Bolognese snapshot generated from the actual published `2e84e74` source. No live learner records are downloaded or changed. The existing JSON progress contract accepts these additive fields; no SQL or backend change is needed. Whole-progress storage still has its existing 2,000,000-byte limit. Twenty-seven new highest-tier Town snapshots occupy about 263 KB before other history; preserving more educational evidence increases long-term payload growth, which must be checked during the full upgrade without deleting historical/pending data.
